# Bibliography

**Technologies for Artificial Intelligence**\
Università degli Studi dell'Aquila — MSc in Computer Science, first year\
Instructor: Fabio Antonini

---

The handouts are complete: every derivation, number and figure the course relies on
is in them, and nothing here is required to follow the lessons or to prepare the
exam. These books are a second explanation of the same material, in a different
voice and with different examples. That is often what makes an idea finally click.

References follow the APA style, 7th edition, in alphabetical order by author.

## Books

Ananthaswamy, A. (2024). *Why machines learn: The elegant math behind modern AI*.
Dutton.

> The mathematics of machine learning told as a story: the perceptron, gradient
> descent, Bayes' rule, nearest neighbours, support vector machines and kernels,
> principal component analysis, backpropagation. Written for a general reader but
> with the equations kept in. It goes with lessons 1, 3, 6, 8 and 9, for the *why*
> behind the methods rather than the code.

Chollet, F. (2021). *Deep learning with Python* (2nd ed.). Manning Publications.

> Neural networks with Keras, by the author of Keras. Chapters 2–4 (the
> mathematical building blocks, Keras and TensorFlow, a first classification and
> regression) go with lesson 9; chapter 5 (generalisation, evaluation, overfitting)
> with lesson 5; chapter 8 (convolutional networks for computer vision) with
> lesson 10.

Géron, A. (2022). *Hands-on machine learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and
TensorFlow: Concepts, tools, and techniques to build intelligent systems* (3rd
ed.). O'Reilly Media.

> The practical companion to the notebooks: the same scikit-learn library, used the
> same way. Chapters 1–2 (the landscape and an end-to-end project) go with lessons 1
> and 2; chapter 3 (classification) with lesson 4; chapter 4 (training models) with
> lesson 3; chapter 5 (support vector machines) with lesson 6; chapters 6–7 (trees
> and ensembles) with lesson 7; chapters 8–9 (dimensionality reduction and
> unsupervised learning) with lesson 8; chapters 10–11 (neural networks) with
> lesson 9; chapter 14 (convolutional networks) with lesson 10.
