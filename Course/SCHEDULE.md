| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|

| Weekly exercises | Set at the end of each lesson, discussed at the start of the next. Nothing is collected: one of the ten is drawn at the exam and you talk through your own notebook. Exercise 10 has no lesson after it, so it is worked for the exam alone. |

| Project submission | To be confirmed with the degree programme |

## Material release policy

Slides and notebooks are published **on the day of the lesson**. The handout, which
carries the full mathematical treatment, follows **by the Monday after**, since it is
study material rather than lecture support.

Students receive new material with `git pull`. The Docker image is only republished
when the environment's dependencies change — see
[`Setup/Docker_Quickstart.md`](Setup/Docker_Quickstart.md).
